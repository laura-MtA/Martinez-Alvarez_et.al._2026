# Figure 2 — Distribution of top defense systems across domains

## Script

##### `script_bubbleplot.R`

Generates the bubble plot shown in **Figure 2**, summarising the abundance and distribution of the top 20 most abundant defense systems across Archaea and Bacteria.


## Input tables

##### `ar53_padloc-sumtax.txt`
Summary of PADLOC-predicted defense systems for **Archaea**, aggregated by taxonomic level.

##### `b120_padloc-sumtax.txt`
Summary of PADLOC-predicted defense systems for **Bacteria**, aggregated by taxonomic level.

##### `ar53_fun_v4.txt`
Count matrix of PADLOC-predicted defense systems per genome for **Archaea** with added information about genome size, genome completeness and optimal growth temperature.

##### `b120_fun_v3.txt`
Count matrix of PADLOC-predicted defense systems per genome for **Bacteria** with added information about genome size and genome completeness.
